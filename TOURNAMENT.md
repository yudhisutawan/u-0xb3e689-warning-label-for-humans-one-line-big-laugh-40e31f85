competition's own judge brief (criteria in order: on-subject warning
label for humans; laugh in one read, turn in the last few words;
recognition of how humans actually behave; real printed-label wording
and rhythm; ≤160 chars, whole line, no filler, no second idea, no

and is the single most recognizable office-life beat in the set, in
